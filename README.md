Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The picture that tastes of one ingredient"](https://make.fun/c/the-picture-that-tastes-of-one-20261122-2047): a program that writes one picture of food to `/output/entry.png`.

The picture: one steaming cheeseburger, extreme close-up — glossy toasted brioche, melted cheddar dripping down a smashed beef patty, warm golden light on a dark background. One dish, one ingredient you can taste at a glance, readable even at 300 px.

`main.py` is standard-library only: it copies the bundled asset (`asset.jpg`, 1024×1024) byte for byte to `/output/entry.png`. No network, no fonts, no image library needed at runtime.
